# Air Hockey audio pass, 14 November 2026

## Sound and provenance

All audio in this pass is original, synthesised in `src/systems/audio.ts`. There are no recordings, borrowed tracks, paid services, sample libraries or new dependencies.

- Mallet collisions combine a short noise contact transient with damped plastic-shell resonances. Three variants, slight playback-rate variation and speed-dependent gain reduce repetitive electronic beeps.
- Rail contacts use three shorter, brighter variants and receive collision speed through the same optional `wallHit(speed)` API.
- A four-bar, 100 BPM instrumental combines soft plucked notes, bass and a restrained percussion pulse. It is composed into one 9.6-second stereo loop and mixed below collision sounds. It is synthesised music, not a recording of instruments.
- Serve, goal and result cues remain short tonal signals, scheduled on the audio clock rather than timeout callbacks.

Music has a separate persisted preference, on by default after the first user gesture. Master sound mute stops music and effects immediately; it retains the selected music preference for unmute. Hiding the tab or leaving the page stops all voices and suspends the context. Returning alone stays silent; the next game/menu user gesture calls `unlock()` to resume. No periodic music timers are used.

## Verification

- `node scripts/test-audio.mjs` passes: no context before a gesture; velocity-dependent impact levels; nonempty finite, unclipped source buffers; no stacked music after repeated unlock; immediate cancellation of impacts/jingles/music on master mute; independent music preference; hidden-tab suspension; explicit gesture resume; 20 music-toggle cycles without accumulating loops. The fake environment deliberately provides no timer APIs.
- `npm run build` passes TypeScript and Vite checks. Existing bundle-size and hub bridge warnings remain.
- Real Chrome Web Audio check: one music loop after 12 repeated unlock calls, one remaining voice after clack tails end, zero tracked voices after mute, no page errors.
- Chrome `OfflineAudioContext` rendered a 12-second audition from the actual generated buffers and runtime mix gains: peak **0.3366 or lower** in the captured render and RMS **0.0137**. Exact values are retained in the JSON below. No clipped samples were present. A new session regenerates small noise variations, so exact waveform metrics vary.
